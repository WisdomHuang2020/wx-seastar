02 / Applications

Different spaces, different light

In the same space, different ways of using light feel completely different. Below are three typical scenarios we serve.

Residential

Living rooms call for atmosphere, bedrooms for softness and kitchens for brightness. Layered lighting (ambient + accent + mood) combined with 2700K warm colour temperature creates a living environment with warmth.

2700–3000KRa>90Flicker-free

Commercial & Office

Efficiency and comfort are the core of office lighting. Linear and panel luminaires with low glare (UGR<19), high colour rendering and even illuminance significantly reduce visual fatigue, supporting 0–10V dimming and smart control integration.

4000KUGR<190–10V dimming

Outdoor

Outdoor lighting faces rain, salt spray, temperature swings and UV. IP65/IP66 protection, die-cast aluminium housings and weather-resistant coatings ensure long-term reliability for courtyards, roads and building façades.
