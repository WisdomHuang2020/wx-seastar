About SEA☆STAR

A vessel for light,
and the craft behind it

Wuxi Seastar Lighting Co., Ltd. is part of Seastar Group (founded in Shenzhen in 1998, stock code 002137). Located in Xinwu District, Wuxi, the company covers a site area of 43,000 square metres and is a National High-Tech Enterprise.

The company integrates R&D, manufacturing, sales and service across residential lighting, travel lighting, intelligent control and EMS. With strong R&D and vertical integration capabilities, it aims to build a first-class IoT intelligent manufacturing facility in the LED industry.

01 / Milestones

Key milestones along the way

'98

Seastar Group founded

Seastar Group founded in Longgang, Shenzhen, covering EMS electronics manufacturing and intelligent control.

'07

A-share listing

Listed on the A-share market (stock code 002137), becoming China’s first listed EMS company.

'16

Wuxi base established

Wuxi Seastar Lighting Co., Ltd. established in Hongshan, Xinwu District, Wuxi, as the Group’s lighting business entity.

'19

Website launch & market expansion

The official website wx-seastar.com went live, with products reaching North America, Europe, Asia and South America.

'20

Malaysia plant established

Malaysia plant established to serve Southeast Asia and overseas customers, cutting delivery lead time to roughly 2–3 months.

'23

Malaysia plant relocated

Malaysia plant relocated to a new 12,000 m² facility, further improving capacity and delivery.

02 / What We Believe

The value of light lies in being perceived

We do not treat luminaires as appliances, but as vessels for light. Our first design principle is always: how does the user feel standing in this space?

Long-termism

We do not chase short-term trends. We invest our time in the fundamentals that truly determine light quality.

Engineering you can trust

Every metric is measurable, reproducible and traceable. We build trust with data, not with talk.

Restrained design

Good lighting design knows how to disappear, letting the space and its users take the lead.
