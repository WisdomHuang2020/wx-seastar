More Panel Lights Shipping from Malaysia

In March 2024, the panel lights (power supply self-made) were successfully shipped from Seastar Malaysia.  Now, the monthly production capacity of panel lightins has increased to 100,000 PCS.

In the face of increasingly fierce market competition, Wuxi Sea Star Lighting officially determined its product development strategy as early as 2018, and gradually shifted its products to overseas delivery.  After years of technical equipment incubation and multiple trial production, most of the products have been delivered from Sea Star Malaysia, this time ushered in the second phase of panel lights production expansion, further improving the production capacity, which is another important moment in the company's development process, but also the company's strategic planning in the unremitting efforts of the members of the departments to further implement the result of landing.

Sea Star Malaysia is an overseas manufacturing center with a global layout of Sea Star Group, with a total plant area  > 10,000 square meters, complete supply chain and management system, advanced production equipment and technical team, excellent process capability and quality system.  To provide customers with high quality, reliable LED lighting, power and smart lighting solutions.  The delivery of the panel lights marks the significant development of Wuxi Sea Star Lighting's overseas delivery capabilities to ensure product quality and on-time delivery.

In the future, Sea Star Malaysia will continue to introduce more automation equipment and experimental equipment, and build a comprehensive and multi-field intelligent incubation platform to meet the growing demand of overseas markets with faster delivery, higher quality and better service.
