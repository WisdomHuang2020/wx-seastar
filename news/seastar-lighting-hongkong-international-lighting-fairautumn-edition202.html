Seastar Lighting Hongkong International Lighting Fair（Autumn Edition）2023

LIGHTING + SOLUTION

Oct. 27-30, 2023, Hongkong International Lighting Fair (Automn Edition) was in full swing in Hongkong, China. This years' Hongkong Lighting Fair attracted over 3000 exhibitors by  theming on "Innovative lighting lights up eternal opportunities" and focusing on series of creative and top lighting products and solutions.

Inheriting concept of "LIGHTING + SOLUTION", Seastar appeared at Booth 3B-B36 and 5B-B39 with new products and creative solutions for expanding partners around the world.
