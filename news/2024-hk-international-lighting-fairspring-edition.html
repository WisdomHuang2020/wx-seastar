2024 HK International Lighting Fair（Spring Edition）

From April 6 to April 9, 2024, the 15th Hong Kong International Lighting Fair (Spring Edition) was held in Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Center, China for four days.  The exhibition is titled "Innovative Lighting with the theme of "Smart Life", more than 1,300 exhibitors will showcase a range of innovative lighting products and technologies.

As an old friend of the exhibition, Seastar also unveiled Booth 3E-B25, bringing a series of products such as commercial lighting, civil lighting, outdoor lighting and industrial lighting to showcase Seastar's latest achievements and new iterations in the field of lighting to customers around the world.

As a professional LED manufacturer, through years of dedication and struggle in the field of lighting, Seastar keeps up with the ever-changing market demand, constantly carrys out technological innovation, constantly upgrades and optimizes products, and constantly introduces new products and solutions, and continues to create a healthier and safer light environment for global customers.

In the future, Seastar will continue to expand its product line and expand into international markets such as Europe, Southeast Asia and the Middle East. At the Autumn Lighting Fair in October, Seastar will continue to bloom wonderful, looking forward to meeting you.
