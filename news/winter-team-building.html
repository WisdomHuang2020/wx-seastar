Winter Team Building

When the warm winter sun fell, maple leaves dyed red branches, on December 24, Wuxi Seastar Lighting Co., Ltd. held a unique team building activity -- winter mountaineering, to welcome the New Year and start a new journey in 2025.

The venue for this event was the scenic Huishan. In the winter morning, the energetic Seastar's members came to the foot of Huishan early to assemble into a team.  All of them were energetic and ready to win.  The breeze brought with the fresh smell of the woods.  After a brief collection and oath, the climbing activities officially kicked off. Everyone carried the "power bag" which was carefully prepared by the company, facing the warm winter sun, stepping into the depths of nature and setting out for the top of the mountain!

The mountain road twists and turns, the team's pace is sonorous. Everyone threw off their arms, stride forward.  Some members were in front of the road, some members were in the back to encourage forward; Some members struggled, some members lent a helping hand, supportted all th way; Some members were physically weak, dizzied and stopped, some members were around, never abandoned. It was not so much a game as a team battle, and taking the top of Huishan was the common goal of everyone.

The winter mountains have a different charm. They enjoyed the beauty along the way while encouraging each other, the mountains filled with laughter.

With the concerted efforts of everyone, all the members have successfully reached the summit, although it has been winter, everyone was sweating. When standing on the top of the mountain, overlooking thousands of miles, everyone's face was filled with the joy of victory and the pride of challenging success, and the exhaustion of the whole body was instantly cured.  Many staff picked up cameras to record this beautiful moment.

In the end, the first, fourth and sixth team respectively won the first, second and third place of the mountaineering team building activity.

After that, all the members went down the hill in an orderly manner and gathered together to enjoy the food. Climb the mountain to the top, the struggle will never stop. This mountaineering activity not only exercises the personal will, deepens the friendship between colleagues, and further unites the Seastar's staff closely together!

December is not only the end of the year, but also the prelude to the next year, Wuxi Seastar Lighting will work together and strive to sprint to reach the top of the mountain and achieve the goal!
