New Launched--FMX11 Surfahce Downlight

Recently, FMX11 Surface Downlight of Wuxi Seastar Lighting has completed all factory tests, and passed customer's verification, the first order successfully achieved mass production of goods, the product team to witness this moment of glory!

FMX11 Surface Downlight is another new product in the FMX series, which has been improved and upgraded by the team members day and night, and has finally been recognized by the market and customers.  The product has the characteristics of both ceiling light and downlight, not only has excellent performance and reliability, but also has the advantages of beautiful and easy installation, suitable for a variety of indoor scenes.

The product development and mass production will further expand the market share of Wuxi Seastar Lighting in the field of overseas LED lighting, which has a positive and important role in the long-term development of the company.

For a long time, Wuxi Seastar Lighting has always adhered to customer demand-oriented, driven high-quality development of enterprises by technological innovation, concentrated resources to improve the speed of research and development and product competitiveness, and shaped the new momentum of enterprise development in China and Malaysia.  In the future, Wuxi Seastar Lighting will always adhere to a rigorous attitude in terms of quality, technology and service, and constantly pursue excellence to provide customers with better products and services.
