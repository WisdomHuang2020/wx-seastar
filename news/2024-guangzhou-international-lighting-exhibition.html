2024 Guangzhou International Lighting Exhibition

From June 9 to 12, 2024, the 29th Guangzhou International Lighting Exhibition (GILE) was held in the Guangzhou China Import and Export Fair pavilion.  With the theme of "Light + Time -- Practice Light Infinity", the exhibition covered 26 pavilions and attracted more than 3,300 exhibitors from more than 20 countries and regions.

As an old friend of GILE, Sea Star must not miss this feast.  This month, the Sea Star team is divided into two ways, one the way to Mexico to participate in the three-day Expo Electrica International from June 4 to 6, and the other way to the GILE.

As an enterprise specializing in LED lighting, Sea Star has brought home lighting, commercial lighting, outdoor lighting and other overall lighting solutions for new and old customers and friends at home and abroad with rich product lines, leading technological innovation and extensive application experience.

In the bright and simple booth of Sea Star, high appearance level, multiple categories of products made a heavy appearance, full of sincerity, an endless stream of customers to visit, experience and exchange, talk happily, and constantly ignite the passion of summer!
