The first batch of "New Design" BPX9 Panel Light is in mass production

Recently, Sea Star Lighting's first batch of "New Design" BPX9 Panel Light has achieved mass production.  BPX9 series is divided into Standard version, Sensor version and Bluetooth version, after the team worked day and night to develop, Sensor version first approved by customers.  The successful delivery of the project embodies the wisdom and sweat of all employees of the company, and is the best commendation for the joint efforts of all colleagues of Sea Star.

The design features of BPX9 Panel Light are mainly reflected in the use of unique horizontal format design to minimize the glare vision, soft light, comfortable and bright, thus bringing a more advanced and healthier lighting experience.  Flexible installation allows it to adapt to different lighting needs, such as suspended ceiling, ceiling suction or embedded installation, mainly used in office, commercial, hotel, exhibition hall and other places.

With a forward-looking global layout thinking, Sea Star Lighting has seized overseas opportunities and successfully established an overseas manufacturing center in Malaysia.  Since Sea Star Malaysia was put into operation in 2018, it has always adhered to the purpose of "fast delivery", expanded its production base, introduced a complete supply chain and management system, and formed a rapid response mechanism for the whole industrial chain, providing a strong guarantee for the timely delivery of overseas customers.

Sea Star Lighting has advanced production technology, pays attention to technology research and development and innovation, is committed to providing overseas customers with reliable and trusted products and services.  Sea Star Lighting will continue to look at the world, seize the development opportunities of globalization, and continue to strive to become a global trusted partner!
