HK International Lighting Fair (Autumn Edition)

On October 30, 2024, the four-day Hong Kong International Lighting Fair (Autumn Edition) was successfully concluded at the Hong Kong International Exhibition Centre.  Seastar is honored to discuss the latest technologies and future trends in the field of lighting with many elite companies and professionals in the lighting industry at home and abroad.

Hong Kong International Lighting Fair(Autumn Edition), as one of the world's largest lighting and related product exhibitions, is not only a bright stage for new product release, but also a bridge for industry exchanges and international cooperation.  With the theme of "Light · Life", this year's Hong Kong International Lighting Fair brings together about 3,000 exhibitors from more than 20 countries and regions, deeply focusing on the innovation and integration of lighting and life.

This time, the Seastar team presented a series of latest products, new technologies and intelligent lighting scene solutions from American and European standards at booth 5B-B37 and 5B-B39.  The booth uses multi-scene and multi-angle design scheme to present a more intuitive and realistic light and shadow experience for the vast number of customers from home and abroad, and also shows the latest innovation achievements and technological breakthroughs of Seastar in the field of lighting for many years.

The exhibition is not only a platform for display, but also a bridge for communication. The Seastar team not only demonstrated the unique charm of the company's products, but also established initial contacts with exhibitors and a number of potential partners from all over the world to discuss industry trends and share experience, laying a solid foundation for subsequent business expansion.  We believe that through this exhibition, we can not only broaden our market vision, but also promote the company's products and services to a new height.

In the future, the company will continue to uphold the spirit of openness and innovation, deepen exchanges and cooperation with industry colleagues, jointly promote the progress and application of LED lighting technology, and create more intelligent, comfortable and beautiful lighting solutions for users.

Next year, we will see you there!
