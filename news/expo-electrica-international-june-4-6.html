Expo Electrica International June 4-6

From June 4 to 6, 2024, the annual Expo Electrica International was held at the Mexico City International Convention and Exhibition Center as scheduled.  The exhibition gathered the world's major mainstream brands in the lighting technology industry, leading the trend of the industry trend, is a very influential exhibition in the local and Central America region.

Sea Star has been deeply engaged in the lighting field for more than 20 years, and has been committed to the development, production and sales of high-quality LED lighting products.  This time, Sea Star debuted at Expo Electrica International, bringing products covering a series of categories such as civil lighting, commercial lighting, outdoor lighting and industrial lighting, and jointly discussing the new trend of high-quality development of LED lighting products in South America with friends around the world.

The exhibition site was wonderful, the crowd was endless, and the harvest was fruitful.  The Sea Star team warmly and thoughtfully received visitors from all over the world, they showed a strong interest in our products, and gave high comments on our products, many customers took the initiative to stop in the negotiation area, hoping to further understand, exchange, and discuss cooperation opportunities.
