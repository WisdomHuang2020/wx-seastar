Glorious Coronation! Sea Star Wins the "Top 10 Recessed Lights/Spotlights" Award of the Alighting Award

Heartiest congratulations! In the selection of the Alighting Award at the 30th Guangzhou International Lighting Exhibition, Sea Star, with its formidable strength, has triumphantly won the prestigious honor of “Top 10 Recessed Lights/Spotlights”! This accolade not only represents high recognition from the industry but also serves as a powerful testament to our deep dedication and innovative breakthroughs over the years.

For many years, Sea Star has been deeply rooted in the lighting field. We have always adhered to the highest quality standards and driven development with innovation, shining brightly in the global market. Every recessed light and spotlight product embodies our unwavering pursuit of superior performance and our meticulous craftsmanship. This award is the best endorsement of the strength of our core products.

Standing at this new starting point, Sea Star remains true to its original aspiration. In the future, we will continue to illuminate every space with even better products and services. Hand in hand with our global partners, we will jointly paint a new blueprint for the lighting industry!
